GOOD QUESTIONS

A little more
clarity.

The details behind a better
support experience.

Does Flowdesk replace support agents?

No. Flowdesk is designed to handle sorting, summarizing, and drafting so agents can focus on judgment and customer relationships. Your team stays responsible for the conversation.

Can Flowdesk automatically reply to customers?

Human review is the default. The product concept allows administrators to explicitly enable automatic replies for approved, low-risk workflows. Sensitive requests and uncertain answers route to an agent. This demo never sends a customer message.

Which platforms does Flowdesk integrate with?

The proposed catalog includes Slack, HubSpot, Stripe, Shopify, Gmail, Zendesk, Intercom, and Salesforce. Starter includes five connections; Growth includes unlimited integrations. Catalog previews on this site do not connect to external accounts.

Can we control what the AI is allowed to do?

Yes. The design includes custom instructions, routing rules, approval requirements, and role-based permissions. Teams choose which actions can be automated and which always need review.

How is customer data handled?

The intended architecture uses encryption in transit and at rest, access controls, and configurable retention. Enterprise adds SSO and audit logs. Flowdesk is a fictional concept, so these are design commitments rather than audited operational claims. This demo uses sample data.

Can we import historical conversations?

The product is designed to support importing historical conversations through support-platform integrations and structured exports. An import preview would let you map fields and review records before adding them to your workspace.

Is there a free trial?

The proposed plans include a 14-day trial with no credit card required. On this concept website, Start free opens a hands-on sample workspace without creating an account or collecting payment details.
